A nurse's note before you fly

Check every medicine you pack against Japan's rules. Some common US medicines can't come in at all, even with a prescription — Adderall is one. Japan's health ministry keeps the official list.

If you get sick here, the Japan National Tourism Organization has an official search that finds hospitals by language and department. Save it now, while you have good Wi-Fi.

If something goes wrong

Calls to 119 and 110 are free, 24 hours a day. Say what happened and where you are. If you're stuck on language, the visitor hotline answers in English.
